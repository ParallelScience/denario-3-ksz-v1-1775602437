- Decision: ITERATE
- Justification: The current project is at a "Borderline/Reject" stage due to a methodological mismatch: the reliance on a Wiener filter and a velocity-reconstruction scheme that are fundamentally ill-suited for the provided dataset's sparsity and noise characteristics. However, the dataset itself is high-quality and the scientific goal (constraining the $\tau-M$ scaling relation) is a high-impact problem in modern cosmology. The proposed changes—specifically moving from a Wiener-filtered pairwise estimator to a direct CMB-subtraction stacking approach—are not merely "technical layers" but are essential corrections to restore the signal-to-noise ratio. By abandoning the velocity-dependent pairwise estimator (which is failing due to shot noise) in favor of a stacked radial profile (aperture photometry), the project shifts from a failed reconstruction attempt to a robust measurement of the gas distribution. This iteration significantly increases the "Acceptance Delta" by replacing a noisy, biased estimator with a standard, physically motivated stacking analysis that is well-aligned with the capabilities of the provided synthetic data. The ROI of this effort is high because the infrastructure for data handling is already built; the pivot in methodology is a surgical correction that transforms a null result into a publishable, high-fidelity benchmark.
