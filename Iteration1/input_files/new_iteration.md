- Decision: ITERATE
- Justification: The current project has successfully moved from a failed foreground-subtraction approach (Wiener filtering) to a robust statistical framework (pairwise kSZ estimator). The detection is statistically significant (SNR 3.78), but the discrepancy between the recovered scaling slope (0.4679) and the theoretical expectation (0.6667) indicates a systematic bias in the analysis pipeline rather than a fundamental failure of the method. The proposed improvements—specifically decoupling beam-convolution effects, implementing optimal mass-weighting for the estimator, and performing a velocity-reconstruction sensitivity analysis—directly address the "Publishability Delta." These steps transform the manuscript from a simple "detection" paper into a rigorous "methodological benchmarking" paper suitable for a high-impact cosmology journal. The ROI is high because the infrastructure for the pairwise estimator is already functional, and the requested refinements are analytical rather than requiring a complete overhaul of the data processing pipeline.
